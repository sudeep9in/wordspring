Wordspring

Read anything. Understand every word.

Open a PDF, e-book, Word document or web page, then highlight, double-tap or long-press any word. Its meaning in that sentence appears right where you are reading, so you never have to leave the page to look something up.

  • The meaning that fitsWordspring reads the sentence around a word and marks the meaning that fits it: “the bank of the river” or “money in the bank”.
  • Works offlineA built-in dictionary and thesaurus with over 147,000 words and phrases.
  • Your word listSave new words with the sentence you found them in, and export them for revision.
  • Private by designNo account, no ads, no tracking. Your documents stay on your device.
